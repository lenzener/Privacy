# Privacy Policy for FileTransferLAN  
**隐私政策 - FileTransferLAN**

**Last updated:** January 02, 2026  
**最后更新日期：** 2026年1月2日

## English Version

FileTransferLAN is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

### 1. No Data Collection
FileTransferLAN **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information**.  
- No user accounts  
- No analytics or tracking  
- No crash reports  
- No usage data  

### 2. Files Stay on Your Local Network
All file transfers happen **only within your local Wi-Fi network**.  
- Files never leave your local network  
- Files are never uploaded to any server or cloud  
- We cannot access your files at any time  

### 3. Permissions Explained
- **Local Network Access**: Used only to discover nearby devices and transfer files.  
- **File Access**: You choose which files/folders to send or receive using macOS standard file picker. We only access files you explicitly select.  

### 4. Third-Party Services
We do **not** use any third-party services, libraries for analytics, advertising, or data sharing.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally. The new version will be posted in the app and on our website.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions, please email:  
apprank@outlook.com

Thank you for using FileTransferLAN!

## 中文版本

FileTransferLAN 非常重视您的隐私保护。本隐私政策说明我们收集哪些数据、如何使用数据，以及您的权利。

### 1. 不收集任何数据
FileTransferLAN **完全不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息**。  
- 无需注册账号  
- 无任何分析或跟踪  
- 无崩溃报告  
- 无使用数据统计  

### 2. 文件仅在您的局域网内传输
所有文件传输**仅发生在您当前的 Wi-Fi 局域网内**。  
- 文件绝不会离开您的局域网  
- 文件绝不会上传到任何服务器或云端  
- 我们任何时候都无法访问您的文件

### 4. 第三方服务
我们**不使用**任何第三方分析、广告或数据共享服务及库。

### 5. 政策更新
我们可能会不定期更新本政策。新版本将在应用内及官网发布。
